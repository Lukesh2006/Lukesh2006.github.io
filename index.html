01 — About

Curious about how machines see and reason.

I am Lukesh Murugan, a third-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Saveetha Engineering College, graduating in 2028. Most of what I build sits at the intersection of perception and performance: teaching models to interpret images, then making that computation fast enough to matter.

My repositories track that path — OpenCV image-processing pipelines (edge detection, morphological operations, Hough transforms, real-time face filters), neural network architectures built from the ground up (CNNs, RNNs, LSTMs, autoencoders, transfer learning), CUDA C kernels for parallel matrix and sorting workloads, and classical AI search (A*, minimax with alpha-beta pruning, hill climbing, classical planning).

Alongside that, fourteen months as a Full Stack Engineer intern at RETECH Solutions taught me the other half of the job: turning model code into something people can actually use — FastAPI services, REST APIs, MySQL schemas and responsive front-ends, with OpenCV and GPGPU work wired in behind them.

Most recently I have been exploring conversational AI reliability with an uncertainty-driven conversational repair system — getting a dialogue agent to recognise when it is not sure, and recover gracefully instead of confidently failing.
